## The project in one paragraph
A recurrent low-rank module (the "reader") sits inside a frozen LM.
- At one layer, an adapter computes a rank-r state u from the hidden state.
- It then updates a latent z for L reads: z^{s+1} = u + ρ·φ(R z + U M Q z).
- M is an associative memory (a decay-first delta rule). A "writer" fills it from the facts in the input.
- A multi-hop question needs several reads, each moving one hop through the stored facts. The recurrence is vertical
  and latent; no tokens are generated.

There are two stages:
- **Symbolic stage:** isolates the mechanism with small readers.
- **LLM stage:** inserts one module at layer-14 `o_proj` of Qwen3-0.6B-Base, and for P5L of Qwen3-1.7B-Base.
  - Memory-only protocol: the facts are only in M, not in the KV cache.
  - Scoring is full-vocabulary exact match (EM).
