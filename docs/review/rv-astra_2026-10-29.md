# LoopLora independent review, round 2

Reviewer: rv-astra. Evidence cutoff: 2026-10-29; DPFP-X outcomes deliberately excluded. This review inspected the brief, both earlier reviews, plans, theory, evidence ledgers, draft, reports, selected result artifacts, and relevant implementation. The local codegraph indexes only `main.py`; substantive code conclusions therefore use direct source inspection. No runtime experiments were performed.

The defensible contribution is now substantial but narrow: **four shared reads outperform an equal-parameter single read in a frozen LM with oracle memory, replicated at two backbone sizes**. Neither learned episodic composition nor a causal chain of intermediate entities has been demonstrated in the LM stage. The next investment should test trainability and the interface between storage and queries, then explicitly train reusable transitions. Further undirected rank, depth, or stability sweeps have poor expected return.

## 1. What the results establish

### Oracle readers and the B8 discrepancy

P1-L/r128 establishes M1-L4 minus B4 on hops 2–3 of **+21.55 pp [20.38, 22.76]**, with positive effects in all three seeds. P5L establishes **+25.78 pp [24.53, 27.03]**. Both use 97.5% paired episode intervals. B8's corresponding gains, **+0.98** and **+1.03 pp**, miss the registered 3 pp threshold; the latter also loses 1.72 pp on hop 1. A positive interval alone is not a gate pass. These are strong architecture comparisons under their registered conditions. [LLM ledger LP7–LP20](../paper/evidence_llm.md); [P1L decision](../../reports/decisions/P1L.md); [P5L decision](../../reports/decisions/P5L.md).

The implementation supplies a sharper interpretation than “unshared reads cannot compose.” `UnsharedAdapter` can reproduce `IterativeQueryAdapter` exactly by copying shared R/U/Q into every step, with identical remaining parameters. B8 therefore contains the successful solution. Its failure implicates optimization, training allocation, or the inductive bias of sharing, not insufficient representational capacity. Shared weights receive supervision through every iteration and enforce consistent coordinates; these are plausible explanations, not measured causes. B8's chance-level intermediate logit-lens decoding is also compatible with intermediate coordinates unsuitable for the final decoder. B8 has 622,594 parameters versus M1/B4's 475,138 at 0.6B; “equal parameters” applies to M1 versus B4 only. [Adapter implementation](../../src/looplora/core/adapters.py); LP10–LP13.

P1L-C replicates the gain on fresh episodes, but final-position-only reads reduce M1's pooled hop-1–3 EM by 29.9 pp. Memory-only removes support facts from the KV cache; it does **not** restrict each question to one memory observation: every question position can read, and subsequent frozen layers mix positions. This is an important boundary on the single-observation theory, not evidence of leakage. The bridge intervention fails both at the final position (+0.39 pp) and across all positions (+0.91 pp). Thus “the bridge moved to an earlier position” is not an established explanation. Other steps or distributed states remain possibilities. [P1L-C, Analyses A/B](../reports/P1L-C.md); ledger PC1–PC4.

### Why learned writing remains weak

RFX's r128 factorial gives the clearest diagnosis: deployed EM 33.8%; ridge storage with learned queries 38.8%; delta storage with true-key queries 39.9%; **ridge plus true-key queries 84.2%**, essentially the clean-value bypass's 84.4%. Neither factor alone meets the rescue criterion. This is evidence for **coupled storage and query alignment**, not a uniquely storage-only bottleneck. High key-top-1 accuracy does not imply the coefficient mixture in the actual read is correct. RFX changes the final-position read only; its nonzero “zero-read” accuracy is not a whole-memory ablation. [RFX](../reports/RFX.md); [raw summary](../../reports/p1l-rfx/summary.json); ledger RX1–RX3.

DPFP improves that interface but does not close it: D128-3 reaches **60.8%** at E16, normalized read error **0.363**, and read-value classification **51.0%** against clean-value classification **90.6%**. The flagged E8 retrain reaches 73.0%, below 80%. Whole-memory zero/shuffle results ≤0.2% establish episodic memory use, not multi-hop use. Values are informative; retrieval still substantially corrupts them; the frozen decoder/interface also leaves a residual gap. These percentages come from different heads or protocols and cannot be subtracted as an additive causal decomposition. [DPFP](../reports/DPFP.md); ledger DP1–DP3/PD1.

DPFP changes feature geometry, normalization and state size together. At r128, ν3 uses **384 KiB**, versus 64 KiB for the ordinary square state, despite unchanged adapter parameter count. Its original-space key overlap is not uniformly improved. The 2.8 pp ν3–ν1 difference is one seed on reused validation. RFX's unsuccessful inference-only normalization does not settle normalization during training. **LSQ never ran**; neither “LSQ failed” nor “capacity is exhausted” follows. E≤r permits interpolation only with appropriate independent keys, not for every learned key configuration. Existing raw-read MSE and Gram penalties were already tried; proposing them again as new supervision would ignore round 1. [Read-loss implementation](../../src/looplora/llm/read_fix.py); [memory implementations](../../src/looplora/core/memory.py).

### Depth, rank and scale

Composition falls rapidly: M1's hop-1–4 EM is approximately **47.9/35.4/18.5/7.2%** at 0.6B and **58.7/41.5/22.5/6.2%** at 1.7B. Training covered hops 1–3. Longer chains require repeated conversion of retrieved values into effective next queries, while the recurrence reinjects the initial question and retains earlier states. Oracle entity values and entity–relation keys are independently random codes: oracle writing does not supply a reusable value-to-next-address map. Error accumulation, interface mismatch and untrained stopping are credible hypotheses; current interventions do not identify their relative contributions. Even the hop-2 bridge decodes best at step 2, not step 1. More updates do not automatically represent more correct hops. LP11–LP13/LP17–LP20.

The symbolic follow-ups refine, rather than solve, this diagnosis. [Symbolic ledger](../paper/evidence_symbolic.md):

- **IV1/IV2:** controlled overlap manipulation changes the depth benefit by 13.98 pp for oracle memory, passing; joint_fact's 3.21 pp misses the threshold and uses selected, rather than experimentally assigned, overlap. Interference is not an established universal explanation of IS.
- **CV1–CV4:** constrained correctness-threshold halting passes for oracle memory, but the continuation-value head and factorized-writer arms do not. The passing arm takes 7.83 ms versus fixed-L16's 3.11 ms and substantially trails fixed-L4 accuracy. Fewer updates is not a latency benefit.
- **TS1/TS2:** terminal-answer losses and the norm-based sticky gate fail; the oracle gate saturates near always-continue. TSM supervises answers at/after the terminal step, **not preterminal successor transitions**. T0→T1 also changes training depth. This does not reject all process supervision. [TSM implementation](../../src/looplora/sym/tsm.py).
- **JS, UN1–UN4, PM1/NC2:** stability regularization reduces overshoot while damaging useful gains; unseen-name depth gains hold within the fixed symbolic feature universe; below-chance one-read errors overwhelmingly select the first neighbor. None establishes LM name generalization or a universal one-read impossibility.

Theory Proposition 3 permits useful amplification with eventual stabilization through nilpotent transitions; global contraction is consequently a poor surrogate objective. Proposition 5's aligned, single-observation indistinguishability argument is narrower than the deployed LM protocol. [Theory](../theory.md).

At r32, B7 fails even hop-1 oracle access. The registered multi-read comparison is therefore uninterpretable at that rank, rather than evidence against recurrence. WP-L6's rotated and shared codes reach 0.1% and 11.8%, both below their gate: fixing the inner bridge code alone did not fix the entire frozen-LM interface under that budget. It does not prove a rank lower bound. P1L-S was not run. [WP-L6 final decision](../reports/WP-L6.md).

P5L adds a second-size replication within Qwen3 Base. It adds neither a scaling law nor independent model-family transfer, learned writing, deep extrapolation or causal mediation. Absolute layer 14 occupies a different relative depth, hidden/output widths change, and hyperparameters were carried over. Its measured B4/M1 inference times, about 0.49/0.63 ms per question under the benchmark, also expose the extra compute cost; they are not universal request latencies.

### Statistical and framing threats

The main intervals quantify episode uncertainty conditional on three fitted seeds and fixed oracle codes; they do not establish precision over future training seeds, codebooks or layouts. P1L-C supplies new episodes, not new training. Reused validation and adaptive method selection limit writer claims. DPFP was an authorized exception after WP-L4; its E8 fallback was already registered, not an unregistered retry. DPFP-X is a separate authorized post-DPFP exception. The Chinese overview predates some final outcomes; final ledgers and decision records govern. Keep old stops intact when registering a genuinely new study.

My prospective DPFP-X prediction is that **neither arm reaches 80%**, with X256 likely improving only modestly and X17 remaining within roughly 10 pp of 60.8%. This is an uncertain forecast, broadly consistent with the preregistration, not an observed result. A pass would falsify it but still require independent confirmation; widening changes representation and optimization as well as state capacity. I did not inspect its results. [DPFP-X registration](../prereg/DPFP-X.md).

## 2. Literature implications beyond round 1

Fresh primary-source searches covered associative memory, learned orthogonalization, recurrent depth, latent composition and supervision. All references below were opened and metadata verified. “M” means relevant full-text methods inspected; “A” means abstract/primary metadata inspected, so implementation claims are deliberately limited. None of the cited sources was inaccessible.

| Direction | What changes the recommendation |
|---|---|
| Effective queries | Q-Delta explicitly studies keys transformed by subsequent updates. This motivates supervising deployed read coefficients, not merely original key similarity. Its architecture is not a drop-in frozen-memory reader. [Q-Delta](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08804v1). |
| Consolidation | MesaNet optimizes local regression; ATLAS optimizes over a context window; Kalman Delta Networks track uncertainty. These support small-support joint fitting, but do not make learned queries accurate automatically. [MesaNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05233v2), [ATLAS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23735v1), [Kalman Delta Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07816v2). |
| Selective edits | Gated DeltaNet-2 separates erase/write gates; GSA2 corrects both association sides through slots. Useful controlled primitives; importing whole architectures would confound the question. [GDN2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22791v1), [GSA2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02816v1). |
| Optimization scaffolds | A recent orthogonalized-read study finds a removable training aid, not an inference rescue. Its approximately sixfold step cost and competitive walltime-matched plain baseline make full Newton–Schulz adoption unattractive here. [Scaffold](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19390v3). |
| Reusable bridges | DiscoLoop distinguishes a decodable bridge from a representation usable by the next loop and mixes decoded embeddings back into continuous states. This directly motivates a new interface test, without importing its task-specific accuracy claims. [DiscoLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00341v2). |
| Supervision | LOTUS aligns latent blocks with explicit steps; CODI distills explicit into latent reasoning. LoopOPD uses deeper teachers, but LoopLora's later states often deteriorate: use correct transitions, not an assumed superior final loop. [LOTUS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31779v2), [CODI](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.36/), [LoopOPD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10623v2). |
| Capacity and novelty | TTT/Titans, DeltaProduct and Sparse Delta Memory explore richer updates or larger states; none directly establishes the best remedy at E16. Recursive sharing and key–value memory are established ingredients. [TTT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04620v4), [Titans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00663v1), [DeltaProduct](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10297v7), [Sparse Delta Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07386v1), [Relaxed Recursive Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20672v3), [KV Memory Networks](https://aclanthology.org/D16-1147/). |

These works mostly train different architectures or use different memory contracts. They motivate hypotheses, not transferable effect sizes. Mechanistic latent-CoT findings also warn against equating intermediate decodability with a complete serial algorithm. [Liang and Pan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00449v2).

## 3. Ranked proposals by expected decision value per node-hour

The ranking is qualitative, accounting for implementation risk and informative failures. Costs are **incremental GH200 node-hours**, including smoke, loading, evaluation and a small debugging allowance. Confirmation budgets are additional. Proposals are for a new preregistered round, not permission to reopen stopped registrations automatically.

### 1. Consolidate the paper around the replicated result — 0 GPU node·h

**Method/rationale:** organize one main table around same-rank B4/M1/B8, two backbone sizes, hop-wise EM, parameters, state bytes and measured latency. Separate oracle composition, learned-writer access, and mechanism evidence. Established KV memory and recursive sharing preclude claiming either ingredient as new; the controlled frozen-LM result and diagnosed limits are the contribution.

Retain the closer round-1 precedents: repeated access to fixed fast weights already appears in [Ba et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06258v3), while [δ-mem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12357v2) couples associative state to low-rank corrections in a frozen LM. The new evidence strengthens the empirical contribution, not architectural priority over those ingredients.

**Prediction and minimal check:** every headline number can be reconstructed from locked endpoint artifacts; no supported headline needs a causal-bridge or learned-composition claim. Compare manuscript claims against ledger rows and regenerate tables from stored outputs. Any mismatch blocks that sentence until resolved. Put negative interventions in a compact evidence matrix, with the exact failed gate, not a catalogue suggesting universal impossibility.

**Code/risk:** documentation and existing artifact readers only; no retraining. The risk is underselling the positive result by presenting every failed repair as equally central. This review itself does not modify the manuscript.

### 2. Establish a learned-writer robustness profile — 0.15–0.30 node·h

**Method/rationale:** before another expensive writer screen, evaluate frozen D128-3 and R128 on fresh T1 episodes: matched templates, unseen paraphrases, and changed fact order, preserving E16 and answer/name support. Include original, zero and shuffled whole memory. This tests the episodic contract underlying KV-memory claims without assuming new storage machinery works.

**Prediction:** D128-3's in-distribution advantage survives fresh episodes, but at least one distribution shift costs ≥10 pp. **Decision:** report paired EM/NLL changes and memory-use gaps; call robustness supported only if each shift loses ≤5 pp and the whole-memory control gap remains positive. Intermediate outcomes remain inconclusive. Freeze the suite before evaluation; use no outcomes for tuning its confirmation set.

**Beyond one hop:** after an independently confirmed writer candidate, register T2 with that same frozen writer for B4 and M1-L4, three training seeds, full-vocabulary EM and fresh episodes. Require ≥3 pp hop-2–3 advantage with positive adjusted interval and ≤1 pp hop-1 loss. Budget **8–10 node·h**. Oracle references must match backbone/rank; X256 has no existing matched oracle reference.

**Code/risk:** extend existing audit/evaluation and T2 harnesses; paraphrase fixtures need new code. Fixed names/templates still limit external validity. Do not silently treat a sub-80% writer as having passed the old gate, or assume its one-hop accuracy predicts independent per-hop errors.

### 3. Test sharing as an optimization scaffold — 1.0–1.3 node·h

**Method/rationale:** copy each successful M1 checkpoint into B8, verify identical logits, then compare continuing shared training against untying for 2,000 steps. Reset optimizer state in both arms. Relaxed Recursive Transformers motivates gradual relaxation of sharing; exact containment here supplies the stronger project-specific reason.

**Prediction:** copied B8 retains hop-2–3 EM within 2 pp of continued M1, decisively exceeding original B8. **Decision:** three paired seeds; retain untying only if its fresh-episode loss is ≤2 pp in every seed and its interval excludes a loss worse than 2 pp. Divergence identifies fragility of maintaining the solution, not an expressivity failure. Equality must pass before any continuation.

**Minimal scope/code:** six short continuations using existing adapters; new checkpoint conversion and equality check. This cannot establish why random initialization failed. It also cannot confer equal-parameter status on B8. A later from-scratch sharing curriculum requires its own matched-budget comparison; do not launch it merely because copied weights work.

### 4. Jointly learn effective query coordinates and storage — 1.8–2.5 node·h screen

**Method/rationale:** train against the coefficients that actually mix stored values. Existing `gram_read` reconstructs `Mq = Σ dᵢvᵢ`, including decay and triangular Gram interactions. Add a directional coefficient loss toward the true fact's basis vector, `||d/(||d||+ε) − e_target||²`, retaining existing raw-read MSE for magnitude and answer/value losses. Directional supervision avoids demanding an unattainable unit coefficient from normalized queries. This is a new hypothesis informed by Q-Delta, not its implementation.

Run a **2×2** screen: delta versus ridge storage; existing losses versus coefficient supervision. Continue the same D128-3 checkpoint for 6,000 steps per arm, holding feature width, query normalization, samples and optimizer schedule fixed. All arms train writer/query jointly; ridge regularization and loss weight are fixed using training-only numerics. Keep the old raw-read loss in every arm.

**Prediction:** ridge plus coefficient supervision beats matched continued delta by ≥10 pp and halves normalized read error; neither change alone matches the combination. **Decision:** the combination is the preregistered primary contrast. Advance only on both thresholds and stable value decoding; ≥80% is still only a candidate until **9–12 node·h** of matched control/candidate training across three seeds and untouched confirmation. No promotion from selecting the best of four post hoc.

**Code/risk:** coefficient diagnostics already exist. Rectangular DPFP ridge storage needs new plumbing: current `ridge_final` assumes square projected keys/values. This is not “run the existing LSQ config.” Monitor conditioning, query norms and coefficient cancellation. Ill-conditioned or duplicate addresses may make target isolation impossible; specify last-write semantics separately. Support-wide regression can destroy overwrite semantics and costs more than one online update.

### 5. Train transitions, re-encode bridges, learn terminal existence — 1.3–2.0 node·h symbolic screen

**Method/rationale:** adapt DiscoLoop's decode–re-encode idea to the rank state, using a shared entity decoder and canonical codebook, plus the continuous residual. Add supervision for the actual successor at preterminal steps; add a separate outgoing-edge/terminal classifier trained with balanced positive/negative examples. An absorbing hold applies only after a predicted terminal state. This addresses what TSM did not test. LOTUS/CODI motivate supervision, not copying their architectures.

**Minimal experiment:** four cumulative L8 arms: answer-only; add successor supervision; additionally add soft re-encoding; additionally add supervised terminal hold. Train on hops 1–3; test 4–8. Use oracle and joint_fact symbolic writers, three seeds, 15,000 steps. Include B4 with identical added heads/codebook for equal-parameter comparisons: 24 multi-read plus six single-read runs. Predictions and inference use no gold bridge, episode candidate restriction or true hop count; decoding spans the global entity inventory. Balance terminal labels across entity identities and training depths to discourage a learned fixed stop count.

**Prediction:** the full arm improves hop-4–8 EM at L8 by ≥5 pp over same-depth answer-only training, loses ≤1 pp on hop 1, and retains ≥3 pp hop-2–3 gain over matched B4. Additionally require ≤1 pp loss from L8 to L16 on hops 1–3: good far-hop accuracy alone does not establish terminal preservation. Require positive adjusted gain intervals and improvement in both writers before a **new 8–10 node·h** LM confirmation. A symbolic-only improvement remains symbolic.

**Code/risk:** existing rollout/TSM harnesses extend; decoder feedback, transition labels and supervised hold require new code. Discrete bottlenecks can propagate wrong early choices, global-code supervision limits unseen-entity claims, and extra heads add compute. Record decode/re-encode latency. Unlike a global contraction penalty, correct terminal preservation need not suppress useful preterminal amplification. Do not substitute terminal-answer distillation for the proposed transition targets.

### 6. Decouple erase and write only if residual storage errors justify it — 0.9–1.4 node·h screen

**Method/rationale:** if proposal 4 shows aligned queries but persistent collateral overwrite, compare the existing delta writer against a GDN2-inspired independent erase/write gate, initialized to reproduce the baseline update. Keep decay and feature dimensions fixed. Defer full GSA2 slots, Kalman covariance, sparse expansion and deep TTT until this smaller hypothesis fails informatively.

**Prediction/minimal experiment:** two 6,000-step continuations, E16, with both ordinary associations and controlled same-address updates. Independent gates improve updated-fact EM by ≥5 pp without losing >1 pp on untouched facts. Advance only if this holds on fresh paired episodes and ordinary T1 EM improves; confirmation costs **9–12 node·h**. Otherwise stop this branch.

**Code/risk:** new update/gate implementation, existing writer harness. Extra parameters and projection cost must be disclosed. Incorrect erase orientation can silently change the memory contract; require algebraic baseline equivalence and update-specific tests on compute. This is a bounded adaptation, not a claimed reproduction of GDN2's full language model.

## 4. Budget and decision discipline

Start with proposals 1–4: approximately **3–4.1 node·h**, then at most 2.0 for the symbolic reader screen. Allocate subsequent confirmation to a qualifying primary result, not all branches simultaneously. A useful first-round ceiling is **6.5 node·h**, followed by a separately registered **≤12 node·h** confirmation tranche. Sequential dependencies and queue time prevent interpreting summed node-hours as wall-clock completion time.

Estimates use DPFP's measured 0.199–0.215 s/step and 78–82 minute full runs; TSM used 0.76 node·h for 18 runs packed six per node. The proposed 30-run symbolic screen scales that anchor upward and allows extra decoder cost. New operations require a timing smoke; preregister the timing-based step-budget rule and release the resulting counts before science. Stop if the cap cannot accommodate that design. Short screens fit one-node, ≤1-hour jobs. Full confirmations may use ≤2 hours only after a positive screen and measured-time ×1.5 justification; stay within eight nodes, charge idle allocated nodes, and include evaluation/startup/debugging in actual spend.

Use pinned cached backbone revisions, a new design lock, and audited unused confirmation ranges. Report seed-level effects alongside paired episode intervals; three seeds do not support narrow population-of-training uncertainty. Fix primary contrasts and multiplicity rules before results. Keep current memory-only access identical across arms, report all-position controls, and account for every added parameter, state byte and millisecond. No efficiency claim against a larger-rank single reader follows from any proposed comparison.
