## 5. Use of the results (prereg wording that now applies)

<!-- quote:docs/prereg/UNS.md -->
> The tables are reported as a scope row next to the seen-pool values. A (writer, reader) result is described as "the depth gain is also present on unseen-pool entities" only if its unseen-pool M1 depth gain on hops {2, 3} is at least 3 pp with the interval above 0 (the project's usual reach criterion); otherwise as "not shown on unseen-pool entities". Part A and Part B are never pooled and are labelled by their sources.
<!-- /quote -->
<!-- quote:docs/prereg/UNS.md -->
> No sentence that composition "holds for unseen entities" or "beyond the closed vocabulary" is justified by UNS: the unseen pool is 256 further entities of the same 1,024-name vocabulary with fixed random features, never used in any training episode, and the readers were not trained to be tested on new codes.
<!-- /quote -->
<!-- quote:docs/prereg/UNS.md -->
> If Part A's audit or reproduction fails for a writer, the committed rows of that writer stay unaudited and are not cited; a failure is reported as it is, without re-evaluation.
<!-- /quote -->

- Wording: all six (writer, reader) results, three in Part A and three in Part B, meet the rule (unseen-pool M1 depth gain on hops 2-3 at least 3 pp with the
  interval above 0; table `uns-wording`), so each is described as "the depth gain is also present on unseen-pool entities", labelled Part A (released M1)
  or Part B (M1 trained at L = 4).
- Part A's audit and reproduction did not fail for any writer (all 18 comparisons reproduced), so the rule that the committed rows of a writer stay
  unaudited and are not cited does not apply to any of the three writers.
- The sentences "holds for unseen entities" and "beyond the closed vocabulary" are not justified by UNS.
