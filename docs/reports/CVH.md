## 1. Registered endpoints and decision

The registered endpoints, the pass criterion, the latency condition and the decision rule, as the prereg states them:

<!-- quote:docs/prereg/CVH.md -->

## 5. Use of the results (prereg wording that now applies)

<!-- quote:docs/prereg/CVH.md -->
> An arm that passes: "a halting head with <label>, <threshold rule>, repairs overshoot for <family> on these frozen readers" enters the paper with its interval; the label-free and P4-S-L failures stay reported. A latency claim is made only where the registered latency condition holds.
<!-- /quote -->
<!-- quote:docs/prereg/CVH.md -->
> No arm passes: the paper states that neither label-free rules, nor a correctness-label head (also with a hop-1-constrained threshold), nor a continuation-value head repairs overshoot in these readers, and that fixed-depth training (B8, M1-L4; P2-S-F1) remains the remedy found. No follow-up halting study is registered.
<!-- /quote -->
<!-- quote:docs/prereg/CVH.md -->
> No gate depends on CVH.
<!-- /quote -->

- One arm passes (`oracle_random/r32/M1`, A1), so the first sentence applies to it: "a halting head with the correctness label (the P4-S-L
  head), a hop-1-constrained validation threshold, repairs overshoot for `oracle_random/r32/M1` on these frozen readers" enters the paper with its
  interval (the A1 row of `cvh-endpoints`, 99.17%). The label and the threshold rule are those of A1 in the prereg's design:

<!-- quote:docs/prereg/CVH.md -->
> **A1**: the same head; threshold chosen on validation under the hop-1 constraint below.
<!-- /quote -->

  The head is the P4-S-L correctness-label head: the prereg describes its label as "the candidate argmax at step s is correct". The label-free and
  P4-S-L (A0) failures stay reported (A0 rows).
- Latency: the registered latency condition does not hold for that arm (`cvh-latency`), so no latency claim is made for it. The condition holds
  numerically for four arms that do not pass the registered pattern; the "Use of the results" text allows a latency claim for an arm that passes,
  where the condition holds, so these results license no latency claim.
- The "No arm passes" statement applies only if no arm passes. One arm passes, so it does not apply.
- No gate depends on CVH.
