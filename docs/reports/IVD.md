## 1. Registered endpoint and decision

The registered endpoint, the pass criterion and the decision rule, as the prereg states them:

<!-- quote:docs/prereg/IVD.md -->
> IC = G(low) − G(high), with G(k) = Acc(L4) − Acc(L1) of M1 in the cell L24-k on hops {2, 3}
<!-- /quote -->
<!-- quote:docs/prereg/IVD.md -->
> Confidence level 97.5% (two writers, Bonferroni). The pattern **passes** for a writer if IC ≥ 5 pp and the lower bound of the interval is above 0.
<!-- /quote -->
<!-- quote:docs/prereg/IVD.md -->
> At equal load, high overlap lowers the L4 − L1 gain by at least 5 pp more than low overlap with a positive paired interval: the interference explanation of the IS results holds (for that writer). Otherwise "interference" is withdrawn as the identified explanation of the IS results.
<!-- /quote -->

Result (source: `reports/ivd/results/interference.csv`, rows with `role` = decision, and `summary.json`; the script recomputes the two
conditions and asserts that they equal the stored `passes` flags):

| oracle_random | 97.5% | +13.98 [+12.64, +15.24] | yes | yes | yes | 0.500 | yes | supported |
| joint_fact | 97.5% | +3.21 [+2.18, +4.21] | no | yes | no | 0.285 | yes | not_supported |

Decision by the rule above:

- `oracle_random`: the pattern passes (IC at least 5 pp and the lower bound above 0). The interference explanation of the IS results holds
  for `oracle_random`.
- `joint_fact`: the pattern does not pass (IC below 5 pp). "Interference" is withdrawn as the identified explanation of the IS results for
  `joint_fact`. The analysis label is `not_supported`, because the achieved overlap gap is at least 0.2 (section 3).

## 6. Observations (not registered)

These are read from the tables above; the prereg attaches no rule to them.

- At L24 the IC on hop 1 is negative for both writers (`ivd-ic`, hops 1 rows), and the intervals exclude 0.
- In the `joint_fact` decay-only cells (`L6-decay`, `L24-decay`) G equals the base cell's G to two decimals, and the L1 and L4 accuracies are the
  base cell's (`ivd-cells`).

  each of those cells is below the base cell's G (`ivd-cells`).
