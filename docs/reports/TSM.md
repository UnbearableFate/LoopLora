## 1. Registered endpoints and decision

The registered endpoints, the pass criterion and the decision rule, as the prereg states them:

<!-- quote:docs/prereg/TSM.md -->
> **stay**: accuracy at L16 minus accuracy at L4 of the arm on hops {1, 2, 3}; the condition holds if |difference| ≤ 1 pp (point estimate).
<!-- /quote -->
<!-- quote:docs/prereg/TSM.md -->
> **far**: arm at L8 minus T0 at L8 on hops {4, …, 8}; holds if the difference ≥ −1 pp (point estimate).
<!-- /quote -->
<!-- quote:docs/prereg/TSM.md -->
