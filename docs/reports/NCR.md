## 1. Registered endpoints and what the prereg calls the decision

<!-- quote:docs/prereg/NCR.md -->
> The neighbour-confusion rate of a (writer, reader, dataset) group is its `first_hop` rate at hop 2 and the share of its errors that are `first_hop`, each with the uniform reference. They are reported in the paper where the hop-2 single read is below the chance line (`below_chance` = yes), as the plan states; the other categories and hop 3 are reported beside them.
<!-- /quote -->
<!-- quote:docs/prereg/NCR.md -->
> No threshold, test or decision is attached.
<!-- /quote -->

The categories partition the C - 1 = 13 wrong candidates of an episode in this priority: `first_hop` (the successor of the query entity), `one_short`
(hop 3 only), `start`, `terminal_other`, `other` (prereg, "Definitions"). The uniform references are the category size over C for the rate and over
C - 1 for the share.

## 5. Use of the results (prereg wording that now applies)

<!-- quote:docs/prereg/NCR.md -->
> A first-hop share above its uniform value says that the single read returns the neighbour more often than a uniform error would; it does not by itself identify the mechanism.
<!-- /quote -->
<!-- quote:docs/prereg/NCR.md -->
> The tables are used only to qualify the reading of the depth gain D in the paper (that part of D measures escape from the neighbour error).
<!-- /quote -->

- In every group where the hop-2 single read is below the chance line, the first-hop share is above its uniform value, and the lower end of its
  95% interval is above it (table `ncr-endpoint`). By the first passage, in those groups the single read returns the neighbour more often than a
  uniform error would; the passage adds that this does not by itself identify the mechanism.
- The rate and the share of those groups are the neighbour-confusion rate that the paper reports (first passage of section 1); the other categories and
  hop 3 are reported beside them (tables `ncr-hop2`, `ncr-hop3`).
- The tables are used only to qualify the reading of D, as the second passage says. No threshold, test or decision is attached.
