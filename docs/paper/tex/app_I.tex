\section{Follow-up studies G2 to G5: details}\label{app:I}

This appendix holds the tables behind Sections~\ref{sec:stop} and~\ref{sec:ctc}, the B8 warm-start paragraph of Section~\ref{sec:lm-oracle} and the SQ2 and LSQT paragraphs of Section~\ref{sec:writers}.
LSR, B8W and CTC are oracle-memory studies at rank 128 on the 768 seen names with one oracle seed; SQ2 and LSQT (with its U control) are validation-only learned-writer screens.
The values of the first three are produced by \texttt{scripts/paper\_tables\_g2.py} from the committed files of each study (\texttt{reports/lsr}, \texttt{reports/b8w}, \texttt{reports/ctc}), and those of the last two by \texttt{scripts/paper\_tables\_g5.py} (\texttt{reports/p1l-sq2}, \texttt{reports/p1l-lsqt}, \texttt{reports/p1l-lsqt-u}); each script checks its key values against the figures the study reports state.
Rounding is half up on the stored value, so a cell that ends in an exact 5 (for example 64.55) prints 0.1 higher than in the study reports.
The evidence ledger holds the exact values (rows LS1 to LS4, BW1 to BW3, CT1 to CT3, SQ1 to SQ3, LQ1 to LQ4) and wins over any shortened wording here.

\subsection{SQ2: ridge storage and the coefficient loss (G5)}

The four arms continue the final D128-3 checkpoint (adapter, training-only value head and optimizer state) for 6,000 steps on one fresh training stream at learning rate 0.003, batch 32 with 4 questions per episode, E=16, seed 17, and are evaluated on the 1,000 validation episodes (evaluation seed 4242) at step 0 and every 1,200 steps.
The ridge store solves a regularized least-squares fit of the stored values on the DPFP features of the keys ($\eta=10^{-4}$, the lower bound of the registered clip, because the numerics showed the error is flat up to $10^{-3}$); the coefficient loss has weight $w_c=1$ (Clarification C1, a log2 rounding of the gradient-norm ratio).
At step 0 the delta arms equal D128-3 exactly (608 correct, all 1,000 predictions identical to the parent's), and the ridge store on the same weights gives 687 correct.  % SQ3

\paragraph{The U control.}
LT-U is the conditional control of the registration: the WP-L4 writer R at rank 128 with its own decay-first delta store, trained from scratch with the unit query on the same data stream, with E=16, seed 17, batch 32 with 4 questions per episode and learning rate 0.003.
The recorded configurations of LT-U and R-r128 differ only in the unit query and the step count (24,560 against 24,645), and the step count was frozen by the same release rule as the arms (at most 2 hours).
It ran after the three arms because the best of them reached 40\% or more.
It is descriptive only: it enters neither registered outcome, and the registered LSQT decision is unchanged.
LT-U reaches 36.0\% (360 of 1,000), with read error 0.589, a clean-value head of 93.7\% and a read-value head of 21.6\%; its EM rises at every checkpoint, so its final checkpoint, the registered selection, is also its highest.  % LQ4
Zeroing or shuffling the memory leaves 2 and 0 of 1,000 answers correct, the gradient clip is active at all 493 logged steps, and LT-U has 475,138 inference parameters and 65,536 bytes of persistent state per episode.  % LQ4
