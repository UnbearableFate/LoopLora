# P1-S validation pilot tables

Validation split only; training seed 17 and evaluation generator seed 4242. Percentages below are candidate-set accuracies. These tables reproduce retained report data and recorded selections; they add no scientific contrasts or gate conclusions.

The plateau flag is a diagnostic: the absolute relative change between mean logged training CE at steps 4001–4500 and 4501–5000 is at most 2% (50 logged points per window). Tail loss decrease (%) = 100 × (earlier mean − later mean) / max(|earlier mean|, 1e-12). A negative value denotes increasing loss. Neither the plateau flag nor step-5000 minibatch CE is proof of convergence.
